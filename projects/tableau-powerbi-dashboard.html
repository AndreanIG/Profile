NYC Flight Performance Dashboard

Interactive Tableau and Power BI dashboards built from the 2013 nycflights13 dataset, surfacing airline reliability, airport delay patterns, and route insights.

Goal

Turn raw flight records into a decision-ready story: which airlines are most reliable, which airports and routes drive delays, and how seasonal patterns affect punctuality across the NYC area.

Approach

  • Data prep: cleaned and joined the nycflights13 tables (flights, airlines, airports, planes) using appropriate joins and data profiling.
  • KPIs: on-time percentage, average delay (departure/arrival), cancellations, distance, and monthly trends.
  • Visuals: bar charts for airline performance, maps for airport delay patterns, and heatmaps/tiles for route-level aggregation.
  • Interactivity: slicers/filters for carrier, month, and airport so stakeholders can drill down.

Highlights

  • Identified the most and least reliable carriers by on-time percentage rather than raw volume.
  • Revealed which airports had the highest average departure delays and how they spread across routes.
  • Showed seasonal (monthly) delay trends across the three NYC airports (JFK, LGA, EWR).

Outcome

Two parallel deliverables demonstrating proficiency in both leading BI tools: a Tableau workbook and a Power BI report, each telling the same reliability story from a slightly different analytical angle. Good practice for turning dashboards into recommendations.

The Tableau dashboard is published publicly; the Power BI report is not shared publicly.
